Privacy & User Data

Nutshl Account & Data Deletion Request

Nutshl is designed with privacy-first principles. We do not maintain server-side user accounts or databases.

What Data Nutshl Stores

  • Local Transcripts & Digests: Stored solely on your device's private storage (IndexedDB / LocalStorage).
  • API Credentials: Google Gemini API keys are encrypted on-device via Android Keystore and WebCrypto.
  • Generated Audio: Cached temporarily on your device file system.

How to Delete Your Data Immediately

You can permanently delete all local data and cryptographic keys directly in the Nutshl app:

  1. Open Nutshl on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Settings icon in the top right.
  3. Scroll to the About & Legal section.
  4. Tap Delete Account & Data.
  5. Confirm the prompt. All encryption keys, databases, audio files, and cached settings will be permanently destroyed.
