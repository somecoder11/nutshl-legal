Legal & Privacy Center

Nutshl Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 12, 2026
Application: Nutshl (Package: app.nutshl.audio)

Executive Summary: Nutshl is an on-device utility operating on a Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) model. We do not operate remote application servers that store your video links, transcripts, or personal credentials. Your data stays on your device or in your personal Google account.

1. Information We Collect and Process

Nutshl collects only the minimum information necessary to execute AI transcription, summarization, and studio audio synthesis:

  • User-Provided YouTube URLs: URLs entered or shared into the application are processed solely to retrieve video content for summarization.
  • API Keys (BYOK): Google Gemini API keys provided by you are stored securely on your local device using AES-256-GCM encryption and the hardware-backed Android KeyStore. Keys are transmitted solely and directly to Google's official endpoints (generativelanguage.googleapis.com) via HTTPS. We never transmit or store your API keys on any third-party or developer server.
  • Local Transcripts & Audio Cache: Generated scripts, audio digests, and bookmarks are saved exclusively to your local device storage (IndexedDB / app sandbox).

2. Third-Party Services & Integrations

The application interacts with the following external services upon your instruction:

  • Google Gemini API: For AI text summarization and text-to-speech audio synthesis. Interactions are governed by Google's Privacy Policy and the Google AI Studio Terms.
  • Google Drive (Optional): If you choose to enable cloud synchronization, Nutshl accesses only its own dedicated application data folder (drive.file scope) to backup your digests. Nutshl cannot access, view, or modify any other files in your Google Drive.

3. Telemetry and Analytics

Nutshl does not use third-party analytics trackers, advertising SDKs, device fingerprinting, or behavioral surveillance tools.

4. Data Retention and Security

All application state is stored locally within the application sandbox. Sensitive secrets (API keys) are protected with native Android Keystore encryption.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

You have complete control over your data at all times:

  • In-App Deletion: You can purge all stored transcripts, audio digests, cache files, and API keys instantly by navigating to Settings > Storage Management > Delete Account & Data.
  • Google Drive Data: You can delete synced backups directly from the Google Drive web interface under Settings > Manage Apps > Nutshl > Delete hidden app data.
  • Web Deletion Request: If you have uninstalled the application and wish to confirm complete deletion of any cloud sync data, you may email us at support@nutshl.app with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". Since Nutshl maintains no developer database, no user records persist once local data and Drive backups are removed.

6. Children's Privacy

Nutshl is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Any changes will be posted to this page with an updated effective date.
